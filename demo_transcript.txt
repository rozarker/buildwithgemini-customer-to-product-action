Part 2: The Bottlenecks — Integrations, Audio Feedback, & Administrative Friction

Ravi: Now let me hear where the friction is. What’s breaking down in your day-to-day workflow?

Marcus: (Sighs) Integrations and feedback loops, Ravi. While Forte 3D handles spatial data like a champ, managing product change requests and field feedback is a nightmare.

Elena: Here’s a real example from last week: Our Lead Site Engineer in Australia spent 45 minutes walking through the module fabrication yard. He recorded a 6-minute raw .m4a audio note detailing critical clash issues between the HVAC ducting and HxGN SDx structural supports. He also attached a 40-page PDF vendor spec sheet and drafted a summary document.

Ravi: How did that get translated into a Forte 3D back-log change request?

Marcus: Manually! Someone on my team had to listen to the .m4a file, transcribe it, cross-reference the line IDs against the PDF specification, manually open Forte 3D, look up the pipeline tag, and log a ticket in our backlog. It took 3.5 hours for a single site issue. Multiply that across 80 active sub-contractors on a $1.8B site. We’re dropping the ball on critical feedback because the intake pipeline is completely manual.

Ravi: What about existing AI or auto-ticket generators?

Elena: They are terrifying. Last quarter, we tried an off-the-shelf automated pipeline script that ingested vendor PDFs and attempted to auto-update our production model attributes in Forte 3D. It hallucinated a pipe schedule class on a toxic gas line! An automated system must never mutate production engineering models without human verification. If an unvetted script changes a pipe spec from Class 300 to Class 150 in the live database, someone could literally get killed on site.

Marcus: Exactly. We need an intelligent system that can take multi-format input—site audio, PDFs, markup text—and immediately extract structured opportunities or model tickets, render dark-mode UI mockups of proposed structural changes, but lock everything behind a mandatory Human-in-the-Loop approval gate before it touches our live production database.


Part 3: Testing the New Solution (Live Technical Pitch)

Ravi: What if I told you we’ve been quietly testing an intelligence layer tailored specifically for this? Let me break down the metrics and capabilities of what we just built:

|                                 ▼                                                 |

|   • Session Artifacts Panel ------------> Dark-Mode UI Visual Mockups             |

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

Multimodal Feedback Ingestion: Your site engineer drops that raw 6-minute .m4a audio recording, the 40-page .pdf spec, or plain text straight into the system. It processes the audio natively alongside document context.

Firestore Native Backend: It instantly parses out entities, line IDs, and structural changes, storing persistent "Product Opportunities" in Google Cloud Firestore in under 1.8 seconds.

Visual Mockups & Artifacts: Instead of raw text, it auto-generates high-contrast, dark-mode UI mockups of the required model modifications or UI layout tweaks, renders them inside a dedicated session Artifacts panel, and hosts them via secure, public Google Cloud Storage HTTPS URLs for your team to view anywhere.

Safety & Governance (Human-in-the-Loop): This is the game changer for your safety team. Autonomous AI subagents are hard-blocked at the API layer. They can draft proposals, flag clashes, and stage changes—but they cannot mutate production backlog items or model schemas without explicit Lead PM approval.
